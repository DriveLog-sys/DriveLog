Help & FAQ

Everything you need to know about DriveLog

How do I post my build?
Click + Post Build in the top navigation. Fill in your vehicle info, upload photos, add your specs and mods — then hit Publish. Your build will appear on the feed immediately.
How does the leaderboard work?
Builds are ranked by total likes received. The more the community engages with your build, the higher you climb. Build of the Month is voted on by the community each month.
Can I link my DriveLog profile in my bio?
Yes — that's exactly what DriveLog is built for. Your profile URL is drive-log-five.vercel.app/#profile. Link it in your TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube bio so followers can see all your cars and specs in one place.
How do I save social accounts from builds?
On any build page, hover over the social media icons next to the poster's name. A save button appears — click it and the account gets added to your Garage under Saved Socials.
What is the Garage?
Your Garage is your personal collection hub. It stores builds you've Liked, Saved for later, your own posted builds under Shared, saved parts from builds, and social accounts you've bookmarked.
How does Build of the Month work?
Every month from the 1st to the 25th, you can nominate any build for Build of the Month by clicking the crown button on its page. You get one vote per month. The winner is announced on the 26th and featured on the Leaderboard page.
Is DriveLog free?
DriveLog is completely free for all users. Create an account, post unlimited builds, follow other enthusiasts, and climb the leaderboard at no cost.
How do I report a post or user?
On any build page, click the flag icon in the action bar below the post. Choose a reason and submit — our moderation team reviews every report and responds within 24 hours.

Still have questions?

Reach out to us directly and we'll get back to you.
